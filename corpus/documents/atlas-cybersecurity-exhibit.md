<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.1
section: C.1
title: Applicability
-->
This Cybersecurity Exhibit forms part of the Atlas Managed Network Services Agreement.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.2
section: C.2
title: Security Program
-->
Supplier will maintain a written information security program.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.3
section: C.3
title: Access Control
-->
Supplier will restrict administrative access according to least privilege.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.4
section: C.4
title: Encryption
-->
Supplier will encrypt Customer Data in transit and at rest.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- page: 2 -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.5
section: C.5
title: Security Event
-->
A Security Event means confirmed unauthorized access to Customer Data.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.6
section: C.6
title: Notification Obligation
-->
Upon discovering a Security Event, Supplier must notify Customer's security operations center within twenty-four (24) hours.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.7
section: C.7
title: Notice Contents
-->
The notice must describe known scope, affected systems, and mitigation actions.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.8
section: C.8
title: Ongoing Updates
-->
Supplier will provide material investigation updates at least daily until containment.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- page: 3 -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.9
section: C.9
title: Evidence Preservation
-->
Supplier will preserve logs and forensic evidence relating to a Security Event.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.10
section: C.10
title: Policy Reference
-->
Supplier will perform these duties consistently with the Orion Security Standard.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.11
section: C.11
title: Subcontractors
-->
Supplier will impose equivalent security duties on subcontractors.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.12
section: C.12
title: Audit
-->
Customer may review relevant evidence of Supplier's compliance once annually.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- page: 4 -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-ATLAS-EXHIBIT-C-C.13
section: C.13
title: Survival
-->
Security Event cooperation duties survive termination for one year.
<!-- /clause -->
