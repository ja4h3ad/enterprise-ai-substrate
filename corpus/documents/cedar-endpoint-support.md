<!-- page: 1 -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-CEDAR-ESA-2025-1.1
section: 1.1
title: Parties
-->
Northstar Mobility Group is the Customer and Cedar Endpoint Operations is the Supplier.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-CEDAR-ESA-2025-2.1
section: 2.1
title: Endpoint Services
-->
Supplier will monitor and support managed endpoint devices used at Customer facilities.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-CEDAR-ESA-2025-3.1
section: 3.1
title: Critical Incident Response
-->
Supplier must begin work on a severity-one incident within thirty minutes after a support ticket is opened.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-CEDAR-ESA-2025-4.1
section: 4.1
title: Cyber Event Reporting
-->
Supplier shall inform Customer promptly, and in every case within two days, after it confirms that an attacker accessed Customer Data.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- page: 2 -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-CEDAR-ESA-2025-4.2
section: 4.2
title: Security Policy
-->
Supplier will comply with the Orion Security Standard while processing Customer Data.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-CEDAR-ESA-2025-5.1
section: 5.1
title: Device Records
-->
Supplier will maintain an accurate inventory of covered endpoint devices.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-CEDAR-ESA-2025-6.1
section: 6.1
title: Personnel
-->
Supplier personnel with administrative access must complete annual security training.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-CEDAR-ESA-2025-7.1
section: 7.1
title: Charges
-->
Customer must pay undisputed charges within forty-five days of invoice receipt.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- page: 3 -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-CEDAR-ESA-2025-8.1
section: 8.1
title: Renewal
-->
The Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives sixty days' notice.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-CEDAR-ESA-2025-9.1
section: 9.1
title: Termination Assistance
-->
For sixty days after termination, Supplier will reasonably assist transition to a replacement provider.
<!-- /clause -->
