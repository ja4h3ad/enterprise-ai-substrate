<!-- clause:

This Service Level Exhibit forms part of the Delta Data Platform Agreement.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.2
section: D.2
title: Monthly Availability
-->
Supplier will provide 99.95 percent monthly production availability.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.3
section: D.3
title: Excluded Downtime
-->
Approved maintenance and Customer-caused outages are excluded from availability.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.4
section: D.4
title: Severity One Definition
-->
A severity-one incident makes the production platform materially unavailable.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- page: 2 -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.5
section: D.5
title: Acknowledgment
-->
Supplier will acknowledge a severity-one incident within fifteen minutes.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.6
section: D.6
title: Restoration Target
-->
Supplier will target restoration of a severity-one incident within four hours.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.7
section: D.7
title: Status Updates
-->
Supplier will provide hourly updates during a severity-one incident.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.8
section: D.8
title: Service Credit
-->
Availability below 99.95 percent earns a five percent monthly service credit.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- page: 3 -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.9
section: D.9
title: Enhanced Credit
-->
Availability below 99.5 percent earns a ten percent monthly service credit.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.10
section: D.10
title: Credit Request
-->
Customer must request a credit within thirty days after the affected month.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.11
section: D.11
title: Chronic Failure
-->
Three missed availability targets in six months constitute repeated failure.
<!-- /clause -->

<!-- clause:
clause_id: CLAUSE-DELTA-EXHIBIT-D-D.12
section: D.12
title: Termination Link
-->
Repeated failure permits termination under Section 9.1 of the Agreement.
<!-- /clause -->
